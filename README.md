### 🚀 About Me

From an MIS (Management Information Systems) background, I moved into Data Science and Machine Learning because I wanted to go beyond describing business problems to actually predicting and solving them with data.

> *I bring a business-first lens to data science — every model I build is meant to answer a real question, not just show off a metric.*

---

### ⚙️ What I Work On

- **Predictive Modeling:** Building ML models in Python (scikit-learn) for classification and prediction problems — from medical risk prediction to signal classification.
- **Data Analysis & BI:** Cleaning, modeling, and visualizing business data using SQL, Power Query, and Power BI to turn raw sales/operations data into decisions.
- **End-to-End Workflows:** Comfortable across the pipeline — database design, data cleaning, exploratory analysis, modeling, and dashboarding.
- **Business-Aligned Data Science:** My MIS foundation means I think about ROI, stakeholders, and decision-making, not just model accuracy.
